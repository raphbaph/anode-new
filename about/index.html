Anode Research designs systems for groups whose hardest problems cannot be solved by command and control.

Decision systems + incentives + coordinationUpdated July 2026

Governance was our proving ground.

Working inside networked communities taught us that participation is not the same as influence, and more information is not the same as better judgment. Attention is scarce. Expertise is uneven. Every reward changes behavior.

We work with teams to make those conditions visible, then design systems that let people act with more clarity and less friction. The work is empirical, practical, and meant to survive contact with the real world.
