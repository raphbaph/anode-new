Governance digest / Rootstock Collective

Rootstock Collective digest,
2026-10-22.

No new topics or proposals; replies in the 1delta, SwaptoX and delegate threads, with a conditional Against vote on the Winks.fun milestone 3 payout.

Window: 2026-10-21 09:00 to 2026-10-22 09:00 UTCPublished 2026-10-22 UTC

Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

New topics

None.

Discussion by topic

[2608 Grant Proposal] 1delta Intents, permissionless solving on Rootstock (topic 929, Grants and Incentives)

  • Achthar announced the beta application is live at x-beta.1delta.io, with an analytics dashboard added to the page. It can swap USDT0 with no gas fees. Thanks were given to Curia, ChronoTrigger, DAOplomats, Tane, iamChekwube and LEGOSI for feedback.
  • IBUKI asked whether the team will publish a simple explanation of how gasless swaps are executed, who covers the transaction fees, and what users should know about potential risks.
  • Achthar replied that an article will be prepared. The mechanism uses erc20Permit (gasless approvals) together with intents, so the solver pays for both the approval and the execution gas, reflected in the quote.
  • IBUKI thanked the team, noted the distinction between the gasless user experience and gas costs being included in the quote, and offered to help introduce the mechanism to the Japanese Rootstock community.

[2601 Grant] SwaptoX Aggregator, Milestone 1 (topic 700, Grants and Incentives)

  • Achthar asked whether SwaptoX would run a solver, or provide an API, for the 1delta X intent solution.
  • SwaptoX said the API and documentation will be public around the end of the month. The standard API is rate-limited to one request per second, and SwaptoX asked whether 1delta needs a higher rate limit or specific features.

[Grant 2610] Recognized Delegate Compensation, September 2026 (delegate threads)

  • DAOplomats (topic 541) voted Against on [2508 Grant Milestone 3] Winks.fun on-chain actions within Twitter. The rationale supports progress on the live DOC minting Wink, but the final payment should follow the guideline holdback: $900 now, with $8,100 released after the 30-day adoption target is verified on-chain, and a resubmission or amended payout structure was requested. DAOplomats also voted on Grant 2610.
  • DAOstar (topic 711) voted FOR Grant 2610, with the rationale that the distribution follows the agreed eligibility criteria and ranking method, and appreciation for the care taken in reviewing figures.
  • ChronoTrigger (topic 627) voted For Grant 2610, supporting ongoing contributions to recognized delegates for keeping a high governance standard.

General (topic 622), Introducing Recognized Delegate Compensation

  • IBUKI agreed that delegates contribute value beyond voting and grant reviews. As a Rootstock Ambassador in Japan, IBUKI sees the importance of local education, developer support and connecting builders with partners, and asked whether the program can recognize these contributions through clear, verifiable outcomes without an overly complex evaluation process.

Sentiment

Supportive overall. The 1delta thread is a constructive question and answer exchange covering the beta launch, gasless mechanics and a commitment to publish an article, with positive reception. The SwaptoX exchange is collaborative between two grantees. Delegate votes on Grant 2610 are FOR. The one dissenting signal is DAOplomats' Against vote on the Winks.fun milestone 3 payout, which is conditional rather than a rejection of the work: the progress is supported, but the final payment should be held back ($900 now, $8,100 after on-chain verification of the 30-day adoption target) with a resubmission.

What to watch

  • Grant 2610 Recognized Delegate Compensation (September 2026): multiple delegates have now voted (DAOplomats, DAOstar FOR, ChronoTrigger For), indicating an active vote.
  • Winks.fun [2508] Milestone 3: DAOplomats requested a holdback and amended payout structure, pending a resubmission.
  • 1delta Intents beta is live; an article explaining the gasless swap mechanism is forthcoming.
  • SwaptoX plans to make its API and documentation public around the end of the month.
