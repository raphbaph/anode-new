Governance digest / Rootstock Collective

Rootstock Collective digest,
2026-11-20.

Discussion on four grant threads including the BitChill V2 relaunch, a SwaptoX milestone review, and a resubmitted delegate compensation vote for October 2026.

Window: 2026-11-19 09:00 to 2026-11-20 09:00 UTCPublished 2026-11-20 UTC

Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

New topics

None.

Discussion by topic

Grant 2611, BitChill: V2 audit and relaunch of non-custodial Bitcoin DCA (gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1022)

BitChill thanked IBUKI for suggesting a more explicit "delayed" status, added it to the V2 roadmap, and said they are excited about the BitChill introduction to Japanese users. IBUKI welcomed the clearer status, noting it helps first-time users understand what is happening and whether they need to take action.

Grant 2601, SwaptoX Aggregator (gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/700)

IBUKI reviewed the Milestone 3 report and swap demonstrations and raised a question on the approval and signing experience, noting Tané already raised a related point. IBUKI suggested bringing the main app, mobile interface and integration SDKs together to make Rootstock swaps more accessible.

Curia Delegate Thread (gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/438)

Curia voted For on Grant 2611 Recognized Delegate Compensation for October 2026, resubmitted because the previous proposal did not reach quorum.

RPCDrift: Rootstock RPC Compatibility and Regression Testing (gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1011)

IBUKI, who is building Rootstock BlockBeat, a community tool that visualizes recent Rootstock blocks and explains transaction details in Japanese, flagged the proposal as relevant to that tool.

Sentiment

Supportive and constructive across the board. The BitChill exchange was positive, with IBUKI suggesting a status improvement and the grantee committing to it. The SwaptoX review is cooperative but carries an open question on the approval and signing experience raised by IBUKI and earlier by Tané. No opposition or contentious votes.

What to watch

  • The Recognized Delegate Compensation vote for October 2026 (grant 2611) is open on a resubmission; the prior attempt did not reach quorum.
  • IBUKI raised an open question on the approval and signing experience in the SwaptoX thread.
  • IBUKI is building Rootstock BlockBeat (rootstock-blockbeat.pages.dev), a live Rootstock block visualizer with a Japanese transaction lens.
