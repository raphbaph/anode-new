Grant [2606] Andromeda Core, milestone 1 (topic 876)

  • Ilichb announced the 4-week observation window is now live. The pilot link has been published on all three official Rootstock Collective channels: X, Telegram and Discord.
  • The X post tells idle RIF holders they might be leaving rewards on the table and points them to the 4-week pilot with @Andromedacomp.
  • The window runs from 14 October 08:45 UTC to 11 November 08:45 UTC. Views entering through these channels will be attributed as official-channel distribution in the final report, separate from organic traffic.
  • The full dataset will be reported at the end of the window: per-wallet conversion, group-level A/B comparison and source-channel breakdown. Ilichb thanked Georgia, Eleanor and the Rootstock team, plus the delegates who pushed the thread forward.

Grant [2609] Kleros Foresight, milestone 1 (topic 977)

  • Delegate Tane supports milestone 1 as a low-cost design phase and says the answers so far cover the market-quality questions well.
  • Tane's main question is what the full $15,000 buys Rootstock at this scale. Strategic-tier grants carry a minimum FES target of 2.0x within 12 months, roughly $30,000 of ecosystem value here.
  • With nine Recognized Delegates and only a handful of grant applications each quarter, the pilot will run a small number of markets. Tane questions whether a participation target of at least 15 delegate addresses issued credits and 10 distinct addresses trading over six months gets near that figure.
  • Tane asks how the proposed framework relates to that benchmark, what the grant buys above the $10,000 Scaling-tier ceiling, and asks that the milestone 1 spec state how much of the liquidity stays on Rootstock after the pilot.
