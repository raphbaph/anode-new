The Rootstock Capital Loop USDC stablecoin ErisProtocol Spread Strategy (t/1036, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1036), General

  • santimirovasergij (critical): challenges the "90% net spread" framing; a 100%+ APR in an amplified vault is not a stable yield and usually depends on token incentives, leverage and liquidity that can unwind. Borrow rates are variable, bridge and smart-contract risk are real, and liquidation buffers can be tested in a drawdown. Asks for a stress-test model before treating it as a Collective strategy.

[2606] Andromeda Core, Autonomous Reputation Infrastructure, Milestone 1 (t/876, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/876), Grants and Incentives

  • santimirovasergij: on the separate builder-prospecting proposal, the zero-exposure pilot result suggests the acceptance test should include reach, not just segment availability. Require named builders to commit before the cohort is frozen, and require at least one distribution channel with measurable delivery, otherwise a second inconclusive outcome is likely.
  • Axia: thanks for the final report; no payment is requested. The conclusion is narrow: the infrastructure operated as designed, but the pilot did not test whether reports change staking behaviour because none of the 18-wallet cohort reached the report page.
  • Ilichb: accepts the narrow reading; the pilot placed no report in front of a wallet, so it says nothing about behaviour change; takes santimirovasergij's acceptance test as a design input.
