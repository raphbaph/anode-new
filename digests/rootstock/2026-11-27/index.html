Discussion by topic

[2610] Predge, disputable USDRIF payments, Milestone 1 (t/1009, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1009), Grants and Incentives

  • predge (milestone update): the verdict signing key now sits in AWS KMS, ahead of the 30 Nov deadline. New attestation key 998912866a4361a5 (ed25519) lives in KMS; the server can ask KMS to sign but cannot read or copy the key. The previous key 3b6551a6439b9bda is marked retired (not revoked), so records it signed still verify; the registry shows both with custody.
  • krngill (support): reads the USD 35,000 as building non-custodial USDRIF job payments on Rootstock. Funds release on a verdict against a test fixed before payment and are refunded at the deadline otherwise; the audit is paid directly to the auditor, contested cases go to Kleros, and the deadline refund needs nobody's approval.

[2611] BitChill, V2 audit and relaunch of non-custodial Bitcoin DCA (t/1022, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1022), Grants and Incentives

  • BitChill (reply to ChronoTrigger): agrees all three points are fair. Backup signer: will work out a multi-signer setup with trusted people and post the final configuration before V2 goes live, ideally before the on-chain vote. Slippage floor: Kann flagged it and it is fixed, the minimum output can no longer be set below 95% of the rBTC amount.
  • krngill (support): reads the USD 20,000 as covering the rest of the Zealynx audit, the V2 relaunch on LayerBank with idle mode, the USDRIF/USDT0 price controls and a year of running BitChill, last payment tied to usage. Likes that the contracts cannot be upgraded or touch user funds and that DOC buys go through Money On Chain.
  • BitChill (reply to krngill): planned changes to the oracle used by the USDRIF and USDT0 routes get the same 14-day notice as fee increases; if the current oracle fails or becomes unsafe, it may replace it without notice, posting the reason plus old and new addresses. DOC purchases do not use this oracle.

  • BitChill: Supportive with conditions being addressed. krngill endorses the scope; the points raised by ChronoTrigger (backup signer, slippage floor) are being resolved by the team before the vote.
