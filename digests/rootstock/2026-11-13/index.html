Governance digest / Rootstock Collective

Rootstock Collective digest,
2026-11-13.

Predge narrows Milestone 1 scope to on-chain verifiability, SwaptoX reports Milestone 3 with the DApp live on app.swaptox.com.

Window: 2026-11-12 09:00 to 2026-11-13 09:00 UTCPublished 2026-11-13 UTC

Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

Proposals and RFCs

No new grant proposals were filed in this window. SwaptoX posted a Milestone 3 completion report on grant 2601, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/700. Discussion continued on the Predge grant 2610, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1009, refining the Milestone 1 dispute scope.

New topics

None. No new topics were created in this window.

Discussion by topic

  • Grants & Incentives, Predge (2610), Milestone 1 dispute scope, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1009

A three post exchange refined the scope of the disputable-payment grant. A P2P trader with more than 50,000 operations since 2019, mostly in Venezuela, asked the grantee to make the P2P scope explicit on top of the audited, deterministic checks for paid API calls and agent jobs. The grantee agreed that P2P fiat trades are out of scope for the grant: whether a bank transfer arrived, or came from a third party's account, happens off-chain, and no pre-committed on-chain test can check it. Milestones 1 to 3 cover only what a machine can check against terms committed before payment. The trader then closed supportive, saying scoping P2P out keeps the grant honest about what can be verified on-chain, that the deadline helper and settlement breakdown should make the dispute side easier to trust, and offered to review the interface page in Spanish once it is live.

  • Grants & Incentives, SwaptoX (2601), Milestone 3 completion report, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/700

SwaptoX posted the Milestone 3 deliverables summary: a React and Next.js DApp frontend is live on app.swaptox.com, described as a next-gen decentralized exchange aggregator and DEX offering multi-path trading across liquidity pools to find the best swap rates. The post received no replies in this window.

Sentiment

Supportive and convergent. The Predge thread moved from a reviewer request to make P2P scope explicit to an agreed boundary (fiat P2P is off-chain and excluded from Milestones 1 to 3), ending with the thread's participants aligned on an on-chain-only scope. No critical or dissenting comments or delegate votes were posted. The SwaptoX milestone report drew no replies.

What to watch

  • The Predge grantee committed to a user-facing page for the Milestone 1 mechanism; a reviewer has offered to test it in Spanish once it goes live, so a page launch is the next expected step in that thread.
  • SwaptoX Milestone 3 is reported complete with the DApp frontend live on app.swaptox.com.
