[2609 Grant] Rootstock Yield Vault: immutable ERC-4626 rBTC vault across LayerBank and Sovryn

  • Category: Grants & Incentives | Author: NeznaniJunak (promoted from pre-RFC thread 894)
  • Link: https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/970
  • Strategic tier, USD 25,000 in USDRIF, paid as four on-chain milestones: 5,000 / 10,000 / 2,500 / 7,500
  • Initial tranche 20%; terminal tranche 30% released only after a post-completion impact review
