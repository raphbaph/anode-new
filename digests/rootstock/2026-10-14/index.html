Governance digest / Rootstock Collective

Rootstock Collective digest,
2026-10-14.

The Andromeda 4-week RIF staking pilot goes live across official channels; the Rootstock Builder Hub LABITCONF proposal is withdrawn, and Kleros Foresight answers liquidity questions on milestone 1.

Window: 2026-10-13 09:00 to 2026-10-14 09:00 UTCPublished 2026-10-14 UTC

Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

Proposals and RFCs

  • Grant [2606] Andromeda Core, milestone 1 (topic 876): the pilot is now live on the Collective's Telegram, X and Discord channels. Georgia confirmed the launch and shared the X post, which tells idle RIF holders they might be leaving rewards on the table and points them to a 4-week pilot with @Andromedacomp to see what they could earn.
  • Proposal [0926] Rootstock Builder Hub (topic 976): GoldenGatsby announced the proposal is withdrawn and will not go to a vote. After community feedback and internal discussion, the team will self-fund a smaller-scale presence at LABITCONF in Buenos Aires on 30 to 31 October instead of requesting Collective funding.
  • Grant [2609] Kleros Foresight, milestone 1 (topic 977): JuanRah answered DAOstar_gov on the baseline liquidity commitment, confirming the liquidity allocation and credit budget per market will be set during milestone 1 before any market opens, to prevent extreme slippage on concentrated markets.

New topics

None. No new threads were created in this window.

Discussion by topic

Rootstock Builder Hub at LABITCONF (topic 976)

  • GoldenGatsby posted that the builder community proposal is withdrawn after considering forum feedback and internal discussion. The team will self-fund a smaller-scale presence at LABITCONF (Buenos Aires, 30 to 31 October), keeping the unchanged goal of giving Rootstock a presence at the event.

Grant [2606] Andromeda Core, milestone 1 (topic 876)

  • Ilichb clarified the FES acronym raised by Kaf: in the pilot FES stands for Framework for Engagement and Staking, the internal name for the structure that tests whether personalized yield reports drive staking behavior.
  • Ilichb updated the delegates (Curia, Eren_DAOplomats, LEGOSI, SEEDGov, PGov, DAOstar_gov) that the observation window was delayed because the Collective's social media calendar was full last week; Georgia expects the pilot link to be published this week.
  • Georgia confirmed the pilot is now live on Telegram, X and Discord and shared the announcement post.

Grant [2609] Kleros Foresight, milestone 1 (topic 977)

  • JuanRah responded to DAOstar_gov's question on the baseline liquidity commitment per market and slippage prevention, confirming the liquidity allocation and credit budget will be confirmed during milestone 1, before any market opens.

Sentiment

  • Topic 977 (Kleros Foresight): supportive and constructive. The grantee answered the liquidity and slippage question directly and confirmed risk controls are part of milestone 1, with no opposition raised.
  • Topic 976 (Rootstock Builder Hub): consensus-driven. The proposer acted on forum feedback by withdrawing the proposal and choosing self-funding, a low-tension closure without a vote.
  • Topic 876 (Andromeda Core): mixed but progressing. A naming concern about the FES acronym was acknowledged and explained, and a scheduling delay was transparently attributed to a full social media calendar; the pilot launch resolved the open question on channel distribution.

What to watch

  • The Andromeda 4-week RIF staking pilot run started across official Rootstock Collective channels on 14 October.
  • Kleros Foresight is expected to confirm the market liquidity allocation and credit budget during milestone 1, before any markets open.
  • The Rootstock Builder Hub proposal is withdrawn; no vote will be held and no Collective funding is requested.
