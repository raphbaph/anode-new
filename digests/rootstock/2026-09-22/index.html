Proposals and RFCs

[2609 Grant] Kleros Foresight - Grant Completion Markets for RootstockCollective (Milestone 1) - https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/977

New proposal by JuanRah. Kleros Foresight proposes a six-month pilot adding a market-based estimate to grant assessment: participants trade on whether a funded team completes its grant (completion = terminal milestone payment). Delegates would receive a market reading before each funding vote. Milestone 1 submission.

Rootstock Builder Hub: a cohosted showcase at LABITCONF 2026 - https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/976

New proposal by RootstockLabs, requesting $20,000 USD-equivalent (Growth-stage, Community & Ecosystem). A cohosted Rootstock showcase at LABITCONF Buenos Aires 2026; delegates/stRIF holders invited to comment before on-chain.
