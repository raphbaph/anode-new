Governance digest / Rootstock Collective

Rootstock Collective digest,
2026-11-28.

Predge revises the 2610 grant on dispute resolution and signer independence; support for the Winks.fun tranche; a clarification question on the 2606 Builder Activation.

Window: 2026-11-27 09:00 to 2026-11-28 09:00 UTCPublished 2026-11-28 UTC

Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

Proposals and RFCs

2610 Grant, Predge, disputable USDRIF payments without an escrow agent, Milestone 1 (Grants & Incentives). Predge revised the proposal in response to a review. Contested cases now go to Kleros first and the 2-of-3 Safe decides only if that route fails, which Predge noted still makes the team the fallback decider. Two of the three signers will be independent of Predge, with no funding or employment link, and all three signers with wallets will be named in advance. https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1009

No new proposals were posted.

New topics

None this window.

Discussion by topic

Topic 1009, [2610 Grant] Predge (Grants & Incentives). Predge accepted all three points of the review as fair, restructured dispute resolution so Kleros handles contested cases first with the Safe as fallback only, and committed to two of three signers being independent, named with wallets before proceeding. https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1009

Topic 546, [2508 Grant Proposal] Winks.fun (Grants & Incentives). Mattsanto supported this tranche. Winks.fun requests 900 USDRIF now, with the remaining 8,100 after a 30-day review requiring at least 12 DOC mints on mainnet from 8 or more different wallets carrying the on-chain marker. The post praised the split as matching what the DAO asked for after the October vote and the marker as checkable on the Rootstock explorer. https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/546

Topic 921, [2606 Builder Activation] Stelios and Mick (Geyser Team) (Collective Rewards). santimirovasergij raised a clarification question before voting: the Q3 update changes the picture because the AON and Prism flows that anchor the application are paused, while Circular Grants use a different process. The post asked whether the Builder Activation would fund maintenance and security of the existing AON and Prism contracts or shift toward Circular Grants infrastructure, and whether the paused contracts remain deployed or can be restarted. https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/921

Sentiment

Topic 1009 was constructive and cooperative. Predge accepted the review, made concrete structural changes, and addressed the independence concern rather than defending the original design.

Topic 921 was questioning rather than for or against. The comment flagged that paused core flows may undercut the application's premise and asked the applicant to clarify scope before voting.

Topic 546 was supportive. The comment framed the request favorably and tied it to prior DAO decisions.

What to watch

The 2606 Builder Activation (topic 921) has an open question on whether it would now cover maintenance of paused contracts; the applicant had not responded in this window.

The 2610 Predge grant (topic 1009) awaits the naming of the third signer and the independent signers, with wallets, before proceeding on Milestone 1.

The Winks.fun tranche of 900 USDRIF (topic 546) is tied to a 30-day, 12-mint mainnet adoption review before the remaining 8,100 USDRIF is released.
