Rootstock Collective digest,
2026-11-21.

The 1delta grant proposal (2608) passed the offchain stage and is moving to an onchain vote, with a community AMA set for 24 November.

Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

Proposals and RFCs

Grant 2608, 1delta Intents: permissionless solving on Rootstock (https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/929). The proposal has passed the offchain stage and is next in line for an onchain vote. The team announced a community AMA on X Spaces, scheduled for 24 November, to walk through the proposal and answer questions before the vote.

New topics

None.

Discussion by topic

Grant 2608, 1delta Intents: permissionless solving on Rootstock (https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/929)

Georgia (1delta) announced that the proposal passed the offchain stage and laid out the sequence: offchain proposal, then community AMA, then onchain vote. The team will go live on X Spaces on 24 November to present the grant proposal and answer community questions.

Grant 2611, BitChill: V2 audit and relaunch of non-custodial Bitcoin DCA (https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1022)

Axia credited the level of detail, specifically the disclosure of the earlier missed grant milestones, the V1 venue failures, and the limits of the LayerBank dependency. Axia acknowledged that V1 demonstrated real user demand for a non-custodial rBTC DCA product, but framed the open question as whether V2 can relaunch with stronger security and a more reviewable operating model, referring to the two-audit sequence and the aim of public code.

Rootstock, Governance at a Glance 26-10-06 (https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/995)

emmarobinson gave constructive feedback on the daily digest, suggesting a small status field for each item (for example discussion, delegate feedback, on-chain vote, paused awaiting applicant, completed) plus the last update date and next deadline. emmarobinson noted that "Awaiting decision" is ambiguous unless it states whose decision and by when, and said this could be done in the digest without changing forum threads.

Sentiment

Supportive with constructive scrutiny. The 1delta update is a positive status move, with the team holding an AMA ahead of the onchain vote. Axia is broadly encouraging on BitChill, crediting transparency and demonstrated demand, while keeping the bar high on whether V2 can relaunch with stronger security and a more reviewable operating model. emmarobinson's digest feedback is process-minded and neutral. No opposition and no delegate votes were recorded in this window.

What to watch

  • The BitChill (2611) thread carries an open question from Axia on whether V2 can relaunch with stronger security and a sufficiently reviewable operating model.
  • emmarobinson proposed enriching the governance-at-a-glance digest with status fields, last update dates and next deadlines.
