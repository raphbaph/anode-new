Governance digest / Rootstock Collective

Rootstock Collective digest,
2026-11-11.

Three grantee updates (Predge, 1delta, Geyser) and two more For votes on GRANT 2611 delegate compensation, with no new topics.

Window: 2026-11-10 09:00 to 2026-11-11 09:00 UTCPublished 2026-11-11 UTC

Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

Proposals and RFCs

No new grant proposals were filed in this window. Three updates to existing grants:

  • Predge, grant 2610, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1009: Milestone 1 progress. The IBUKI testnet PoC went live on 9 November on Rootstock testnet, using the unchanged v2.1 job escrow (AgentJobV2, 0x5eAe41E4a082e6F5fDda067535757927c2716844) plus a minimal no-objection evaluator adapter (0xc1e3259a417FD06781941cA973dB6e58274DA683). Both verified on Sourcify (exact match) and the end-to-end smoke test passed.
  • 1delta, grant 2608, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/929: article on gasless swaps published. Intents let users trade on Rootstock with no BTC in their wallet; a filler does the trade and pays the gas.
  • Geyser, builder activation 2606, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/921: Q3 review and forward plan. On 3 August the Boltz swap service the Bitcoin-Rootstock payment infrastructure relied on went down, interrupting payments across Geyser; the team shared the resulting decisions.

None. No new topics were created in this window.
