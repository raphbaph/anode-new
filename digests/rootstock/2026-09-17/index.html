Update - #2609 Rootstock Yield Vault

[2609 Grant] Rootstock Yield Vault: immutable ERC-4626 rBTC vault across LayerBank and Sovryn (Grants & Incentives). https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/970

Two new posts by proposer NeznaniJunak: an answer to delegate scrutiny plus a formal edit record.

  • Economics defended: venue rates are low (LayerBank 0.0025% APR, Sovryn 0.92%, read from contracts 10 Sep) and no gate requires them to rise. USD 15,000 is Coinspect's fixed fee passed through at cost; up to USD 10,000 is the proposer's own work (two deployments, 90 days).
  • M4 redesigned after @Kaf_Anode's critique: it had gated the whole $7,500 on a day-90 number the proposer conceded was wrong. Now nothing below $10,000 TVL, otherwise $7,500 x TVL / $50,000, capped at $7,500.
  • Edit record posted 16 Sep (opening post's edit window closed): where the two differ, this post governs. Adds a dated Blockscout pilot paragraph under Demand validation.
  • TVL will be read from contracts at a named block, priced at the CMC close, proposer-controlled wallets excluded; figures posted in-thread before any on-chain M4 is filed.

Sentiment: mixed but constructive; delegate pushback absorbed into the proposal text rather than resisted.
