Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

Proposals and RFCs

No new proposals or RFCs were posted this window. Three existing grant threads received updates in Grants and Incentives, and a delegate vote was recorded on the Recognized Delegate Compensation resubmission.

New topics

None.

Discussion by topic

[2508 Grant Proposal] Winks.fun: On-chain actions on Rootstock within Twitter (https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/546)

emmarobinson argued that the chain-verifiable marker answers the measurement concern but not the release structure. If Grant Guidelines V3.3 applies, the M3 request should be amended to USD 900 on approval and USD 8,100 after the T+30 review, since a public commitment does not give the DAO a clawback if the target is missed. The team was asked to confirm whether the M3 request will be revised.

[2611 Grant] BitChill: V2 audit and relaunch of non-custodial Bitcoin DCA (https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1022)

BitChill responded to Axia and santimirovasergij, agreeing to make its commitments part of the record. Since the proposal can no longer be edited, the commitments are restated in the thread and will be repeated in the M1 on-chain proposal and in each milestone's evidence: M4 volume excludes purchases funded by the applicant, the admin Safe, the grant wallet or any other wallet the applicant controls. Curia asked whether a backup plan exists if BitChill is unavailable for an extended period; the infrastructure is documented, but the question is whether another person would have the access and resources to take over the keeper.

[2610 Grant] Predge, disputable USDRIF payments on Rootstock without an escrow agent, Milestone 1 (https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1009)

santimirovasergij noted that job 1 used tRBTC while USDRIF is the grant's default settlement asset, and asked whether job 2, the objection branch or another PoC run will use testnet USDRIF end to end. That would exercise the USDRIF path before mainnet and reveal any testnet liquidity or token setup needs; if USDRIF is not available on Rootstock testnet, the delegate asked that the constraint be noted.

Recognized Delegate Compensation, October 2026 Resubmission (GRANT 2611) (https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/711)

DAOstar_gov recorded a FOR vote in the DAOstar Delegate Thread. The rationale states the proposal is a duplicate of the prior Grant 2611 and was resubmitted because it did not meet quorum; DAOstar supports strong governance and rewarding delegates who support that goal.

Sentiment

Mixed to constructive overall. The Winks.fun thread carries a substantive objection on the M3 release structure under Grant Guidelines V3.3, with the team asked to confirm a revision. The BitChill thread is cooperative, with the grantee accepting prior feedback, while Curia's question flags key-person risk around the keeper. The Predge thread is a constructive delegate question on exercising the USDRIF path, and the DAOstar vote is supportive of the compensation resubmission.

What to watch

  • The Winks.fun team was asked to confirm whether the M3 request will be revised to USD 900 on approval and USD 8,100 after the T+30 review.
  • BitChill committed to repeating its commitments in the M1 on-chain proposal; Curia's question on a keeper backup plan awaits a response.
  • santimirovasergij asked whether a future Predge PoC run will use testnet USDRIF end to end; the question awaits an answer.
  • The Recognized Delegate Compensation, October 2026 resubmission continues to collect delegate votes after missing quorum on its first pass.
