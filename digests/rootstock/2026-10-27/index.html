Proposals and RFCs

Carl posted a new grant proposal, RPCDrift: Rootstock RPC Compatibility and Regression Testing, in Grants and Incentives: https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1011. It is an open-source developer toolkit to help applications and infrastructure providers identify RPC compatibility issues before they affect production systems on Rootstock, in the category Developer Tooling and Infrastructure.

New topics

  • RPCDrift: Rootstock RPC Compatibility and Regression Testing (Grants and Incentives), by Carl: https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1011

Discussion by topic

  • Topic 1009, [2610 Grant] Predge, Milestone 1 (Grants and Incentives):
  • predge replied to IBUKI on how disputes are handled. The checks are deliberately boring and objective: HTTP status, content type, required fields, a JSON schema, an expected file hash, a time limit. Both sides see the exact test before any money moves.
  • Seviramar was supportive, appreciating the transparency that separates what exists from what does not and the disclosure of incidents and pre-revenue status. Seviramar called the underlying problem (no recourse when a final payment on Rootstock is not fulfilled) real, and noted the design with non-upgradeable contracts, on-chain caps, deadline refunds and an audit.
  • IBUKI shared a practical use case from a Japan-based demolition business: repeated payment delays between companies even after agreed work was completed. IBUKI suggested it could be useful for a future template, with payments divided into phases in construction and demolition contracts.

Sentiment

Supportive and constructive. Seviramar and IBUKI both engage positively with the Predge proposal, and IBUKI adds a real-world use case rather than raising an objection. The new RPCDrift proposal has no discussion yet.

What to watch

  • Grant 2610 (Predge) Milestone 1 continues to draw supportive engagement; the milestone deliverable is tracked against the acceptance criteria.
  • The RPCDrift proposal (topic 1011) is new and open for comment.
  • No votes opening or closing in this window.
