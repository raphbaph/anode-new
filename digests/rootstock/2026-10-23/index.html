Rootstock Collective digest,
2026-10-23.

New Predge grant proposal for a payments verdict layer; delegates report FOR votes on September delegate compensation, with a question on a 3,500 versus 3,750 transfer discrepancy.

Window: 2026-10-22 09:00 to 2026-10-23 09:00 UTCPublished 2026-10-23 UTC

Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

Proposals and RFCs

  • New grant proposal, topic 1009: [Grant Proposal] Predge, a verdict layer so Rootstock payments can be disputed without an escrow agent (Grants & Incentives). Requested 30,000 USD equivalent, milestone-gated in three tranches. Applicant Predge (predge.io), a solo founder; individual KYC submitted via Persona on 7 Oct 2026, pending confirmation. Prior Rootstock work: rsk-l402, an L402 to x402 bridge adapter shipped August 2026. https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1009
  • Grant 2610, Recognized Delegate Compensation for September 2026: Axia (topic 694), Curia (topic 438) and Tane (topic 463) each reported a FOR vote.

New topics

  • [Grant Proposal] Predge, a verdict layer so Rootstock payments can be disputed without an escrow agent (Grants & Incentives, 2 posts). https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1009

Discussion by topic

  • Grant 2610 (delegate votes): Axia voted FOR the payments to September-qualified delegates. Curia voted FOR, supporting continuation of the compensation program. Tane voted FOR, following the posted September results, but noted the executable transfers 3,500 USDRIF while the results table totals 3,750 and asked Anode to confirm how the difference will be addressed. Tane disclosed being a beneficiary of the compensation.
  • Topic 622, Introducing Recognized Delegate Compensation: Curia said event-related initiatives are not a current priority. Raphael_Anode (Anode) said anything that increases staking and vault usage should have a strong case for a share of growth. IBUKI proposed a small pilot to connect local education with measurable ecosystem outcomes such as new users exploring Rootstock applications.
  • Topic 929, [2608 Grant Proposal] 1delta Intents: Tane asked how the per-user cap of about 1,000 USDRIF and the total cap are enforced before further promotion, including a raffle and official announcement. Achthar said the cap is enforced by the UI, with a warning under the input amount, and an overall fill limit of 25,000 USD applied the same way.
  • Topic 94, General Guidelines for Grant Applications: Nihar_Areta_Market said audit support has become an unsolved pain point, with teams struggling to collect quotes from security firms and delegates sifting through what seems reasonable case by case.
  • Topic 1009, Predge: the proposal post, followed by an update for delegates with two corrections: already running on Rootstock testnet since August, source at github.com/predgeAI/predge-rootstock, with contracts PredgeAgentValidator 0x7041Ed7B6D33F6A8D2605b120194645765d67a85, AgentJob 0x2Bf0EaAc5FA4EaF36AA3a55883F6ba642385690E and VerdictAnchor 0xDCf119ACd13d89F2926fF792070c6dA4d4134356; one verdict batch has been anchored end to end.
