Reserve Protocol digest,
2026-11-25.

No new topics. Replies continued in the DTF deprecation RFCs and the eUSD revenue share update, covering redemption mechanics and operational sequencing.

Window: 2026-11-24 09:15 to 2026-11-25 09:15 UTCPublished 2026-11-25 UTC

Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

Proposals and RFCs

No new RFC or implementation proposal opened in this window. Activity continued inside the existing DTF deprecation RFC series and one proposal-update thread.

  • [RFC] Deprecating DGI (topic 1768): Raphael_Anode (Anode) clarified that redemption does not route through Uniswap. redeem() transfers the basket tokens directly from the DTF contract to the redeemer, pro rata of the basket, with no swap and no pool. The pool matters only for buying and selling DGI on the secondary market.
  • [RFC] Deprecating BGCI (topic 1769): Raphael_Anode (Anode) said redemption and holders are unaffected and no exit window is needed. deprecateFolio() sets isDeprecated to true and voids the TVL fee; the notDeprecated guard applies only to minting, starting a rebalance, opening an auction, bidding and deploying a trusted filler, so redeem() carries no deprecation guard.
  • [RFC] Deprecating DFX (topic 1767): sarahrobinson called the proposal clear, with one operational point before step 1: verify that the redemption path in the Reserve app does not depend on any role being removed and test a small redemption, and consider notice to the 88 holders and Uniswap V3 LPs that mints and rebalances stop while redemption remains available.
  • [RFC] Deprecating CLX (topic 1766): gatherszoellner0500 raised whether a final rebalance before deprecateFolio would improve the redemption basket for remaining holders, noting the current 50/29/15 split is concentrated and is fixed once rebalancing is blocked.
  • [RFC] eUSD Revenue Share Programme Update 08-10-2026 (topic 1664): gatherszoellner0500 asked whether the planned automation calculates the 3% relative change at each biweekly snapshot and auto-skips sub-threshold proposals, or still requires a manual check.
