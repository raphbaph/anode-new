Governance digest / Reserve Protocol

Reserve Protocol digest,
2026-11-21.

UGLYCASH announced eUSD reserves moving from Base to Ethereum mainnet; discussion continued on the open financial marketplace proposal.

Window: 2026-11-20 09:15 to 2026-11-21 09:15 UTCPublished 2026-11-21 UTC

Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

Proposals and RFCs

  • [Discussion] From DTFs to an Open Financial Marketplace: A Proposal for Reserve and UGLYCASH (topic 1679, Ideas and suggestions) received a new reply. sarahrobinson agreed that a 50-token index menu does not prove demand, but argued the permissionless marketplace needs a clear risk boundary: anyone can define a DTF while independent lending markets decide collateral eligibility, oracle design, LTV and liquidation rules; otherwise a weak portfolio becomes a stablecoin risk. The post suggested the better test is whether any DTF attracts repeat users rather than how many launch.

No new formal RFC or implementation proposal was posted in this window.

New topics

  • [Electronic Dollar (eUSD)] UGLYCASH is migrating its eUSD reserves from Base to Ethereum mainnet (topic 1776), opened by karina.abdelmalek on 2026-11-20. Reason for the move: eUSD liquidity pools on Base are being sunset, so swapping eUSD there is no longer cost effective; and all eUSD issuance and redemption happens on mainnet, so UGLYCASH wants its reserves where the protocol operates.

Discussion by topic

  • Topic 1776 (eUSD reserve migration). The opening post by karina.abdelmalek set out the two reasons above. A follow-up by kolodzikearnest9770 asked whether revenue-share snapshots will track mainnet wallets from the moment the pre-funded eUSD is added or only after each bridged batch lands, and flagged a double-counting risk if Base wallet balances, in-transit bridge amounts and mainnet balances are all visible during the five-day migration window.
  • Topic 1679 (open financial marketplace). sarahrobinson responded as described in Proposals and RFCs, pressing for a defined risk boundary around collateral, oracle and liquidation authority, and pointing to repeat user adoption as a stronger demand signal than the number of DTFs launched.

Sentiment

On topic 1776 the discussion is informational, with a technical clarifying question on revenue-share snapshot timing rather than opposition. On topic 1679 the tone is engaged but critical-constructive, supporting the marketplace direction while calling for a clear risk boundary.

What to watch

  • The five-day migration window for eUSD reserves, during which Base wallet balances, in-transit bridge amounts and mainnet balances overlap.
  • The open question on whether revenue-share snapshots begin when pre-funded eUSD is added or when each bridged batch lands.
  • Ongoing discussion on the open financial marketplace proposal (topic 1679).
