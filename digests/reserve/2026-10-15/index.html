R72 endorsed the RFC to adopt the eUSD methodology for USD3 and posted a monthly delegate activity summary.

Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

No new RFCs or implementation proposals opened in this window. The existing RFC on adopting the eUSD methodology for USD3 and changing the collateral basket received a further supportive reply from R72.

None.
