Latest edition: 2026-11-27

Proposals and RFCs

Raphael_Anode (Anode) opened a new topic aggregating the three rounds of DTF deprecations run in 2026, plus the October governance upgrades, into one timeline (https://forum.reserve.org/t/1780). It records which DTFs were wound down, the order the on-chain steps run in, and what is still open for holders to vote on, linking full contract mechanics and redemption instructions to the DTF deprecation and redemption guide.

On the ABX proposal-spam protection upgrade IP (https://forum.reserve.org/t/1667), Raphael_Anode (Anode) flagged three stale items in the thread: the reward payout names "vlSQUILL-ALTT" while the steps in the same post say "vlALTT-OPEN" (copy/paste from the OPEN upgrade), and a closing instruction still telling users to wait until Day 8 to unstake.

On the RFC to add the UglyCash address to eUSD revenue share (https://forum.reserve.org/t/1678), Raphael_Anode (Anode) clarified the intent is forward facing. Adding wallets requires no on-chain IP because the revenue split is calculated off-chain, and a post by an official FinTech member (UGLYCASH's Kari) is enough to include the wallet. The revenue share will be automated soon, after which wallet changes would likely require an IP.

New topics

  • DTF deprecation class of 2026: wind-downs and open items (Governance and Delegates) by Raphael_Anode (Anode), https://forum.reserve.org/t/1780.

Discussion by topic

DTF deprecations (1780): the new topic frames the aggregated timeline and what remains open to vote on.

ABX proposal-spam IP (1667): outdated copy flagged, namely a reward-name label, a copy/paste from the OPEN upgrade, and a Day 8 unstake instruction.

UglyCash address RFC (1678): the retroactive versus prospective question was answered; the intent is forward facing.

UGLYCASH eUSD migration (1776): karina.abdelmalek clarified that during the migration only UGLYCASH's own balances count for the snapshots, with no double-counting between Base, in-transit and mainnet. The methodology will be detailed in the next revenue share RFC.

R72 Delegate Platform (1481): sarahrobinson suggested the eUSD dashboard show the actual revenue split between FinTechs and RSR stakers alongside the agreed percentages, plus the effective yield each side receives, so stakers can verify the programme without manual calculations. The question is whether the dashboard pulls data on-chain or from an internal script.

Sentiment

Supportive and constructive across the window. Activity consisted of clarifications, a request for dashboard transparency, and a new tracking thread, with no opposition or contested votes.

What to watch

  • The DTF deprecation tracker (1780) is the reference for which DTFs are wound down, on-chain sequencing, and what remains to be voted on.
  • The ABX IP (1667) thread contains flagged stale copy (a reward name and a Day 8 unstake instruction).
  • UGLYCASH is migrating eUSD reserves from Base to Ethereum mainnet; snapshot methodology is due in the next revenue share RFC.
  • eUSD revenue share wallet additions carry no on-chain IP requirement while the split is calculated off-chain; automation may change that.
