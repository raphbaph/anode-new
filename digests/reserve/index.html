Discussion by topic

Zeb Delegate Platform (topic 1478)

zeb reported housekeeping proposals across CMC20, PHOTON, POWER, NEOCLOUD, ROBOTS, BUILDOUT and MAG7 upgraded to v1.1.0, and noted that the Shared vlRSR Proposal Thresholds and DTF 1% proposalThreshold proposals are live. https://forum.reserve.org/t/1478

0xd15c0 Delegate Platform (topic 1479)

0xd15co reported October 28 delegate compensation of 136,538 RSR (USD 196.98) with no sale, consistent with a stated commitment not to sell compensation and to stake or invest it into a DTF. For November, the platform recorded an AGAINST vote on the eUSD Revenue Share Programme Update of 05-11-2026. https://forum.reserve.org/t/1479

Sentiment

The four deprecation RFCs are newly posted with no replies yet. No delegate votes or objections were raised in this window. The two platform posts are informational updates.

What to watch

The deprecation RFCs for BGCI, DGI, DFX and CLX are open for community comment before any onchain transition. Voting remains live for the DTF governance 1.1.0 upgrades and the vlRSR and DTF 1% proposalThreshold proposals, which require 10% quorum to pass.
