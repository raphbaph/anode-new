Reserve Protocol digest,
2026-11-11.

Voting is live for the CMC20 and ixEdel optimistic governance upgrades, six DTF governance 1.1.0 upgrades, and vlRSR 1% proposalThreshold proposals.

Window: 2026-11-10 09:15 to 2026-11-11 09:15 UTCPublished 2026-11-11 UTC

Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

Proposals and RFCs

CMC20 + ixEdel optimistic governance upgrade (1.1.0) (topic 1694)

tbrent announced the ixEdel proposal is live and the CMC20 proposal is live, both to be voted on in the Reserve app under DTFs. https://forum.reserve.org/t/1694

Proposal-spam protection upgrade for vlRSR governance (topic 1661)

tbrent confirmed the second round of proposals is live for PHOTON, NEOCLOUD, POWER, BUILDOUT, ROBOTS and MAG7. These upgrade the DTF governance systems, not the DAO or staking vault side which is already done, from 1.0.0 to 1.1.0. The upgrades are in-place. https://forum.reserve.org/t/1661

Shared vlRSR Proposal Thresholds (topic 1695)

tbrent announced the vlRSR 1% proposalThreshold proposals are live on Mainnet, Base and BSC. The Mainnet proposal is not currently used by any vaults, though ixEdel will use it soon. The DTF 1% proposalThresholds proposals are also live for PHOTON, NEOCLOUD, POWER, BUILDOUT, ROBOTS and MAG7. Other DTF governors do not need the 1% proposal bump, as their 1.1.0 upgrades include 1% by default. https://forum.reserve.org/t/1695

New topics

No new topics in this window.

Discussion by topic

Proposal-spam protection upgrade for vlRSR governance (topic 1661)

zeb asked whether these are proposals governors need to actively vote on and whether governors hold voting power on them, noting it would be a first vote since the optimistic governance change. tbrent replied that the optimistic path is only enabled for rebalance proposals, so this is a normal pessimistic proposal that needs the normal 10% quorum. https://forum.reserve.org/t/1661

Sentiment

Discussion was procedural and informational. One governor asked how voting works under the new optimistic governance regime and tbrent clarified the DTF upgrades are standard pessimistic proposals. No opposition was raised.

What to watch

Voting is open for the ixEdel and CMC20 proposals, six DTF governance 1.1.0 upgrades and the vlRSR and DTF 1% proposalThreshold proposals. The six DTF upgrades need 10% quorum to pass.
