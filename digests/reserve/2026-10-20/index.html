Proposals and RFCs

[RFC] Adoption of the eUSD Methodology for USD3 and Collateral Basket Change (withdrawn)

https://forum.reserve.org/t/1662

ham, the author, withdrew this RFC. After Nevin discussed the upcoming depreciation of USD3 on the community call, ham decided not to progress the RFC further and thanked those who participated in the discussion.

[RFC] Proposal-spam protection upgrade for OPEN

https://forum.reserve.org/t/1666

tbrent replied to a question on the OPEN proposal-spam protection upgrade. Standard proposals become rate-limited, and a rotation is required for vlSQUILL holders that also makes it possible to do SQUILL rewards. Asked whether the upgrade can launch with a 5% proposal threshold and 15% quorum across Basket, DTF, and DAO governance, tbrent confirmed yes, and pointed to a new candidate vault with this configuration.
