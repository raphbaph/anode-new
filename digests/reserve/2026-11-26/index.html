Reserve Protocol digest,
2026-11-26.

No new topics. ABX proposal-spam upgrade executed; rebalancing polls opened for CLX, DFX, BGCI and DGI deprecations; eUSD revenue share threads updated.

Window: 2026-11-25 09:15 to 2026-11-26 09:15 UTCPublished 2026-11-26 UTC

Independent daily summary of public forum activity, compiled by Anode Research. Not an official communication of Reserve or Rootstock Collective. Anode participates in both as a delegate.

Proposals and RFCs

The ABX proposal-spam protection upgrade (Governance and Delegates, https://forum.reserve.org/t/1667) is complete. tbrent confirmed both proposals passed and executed. A reviewer flagged a copy/paste typo ("vlSQUILL-ALTT" instead of "vlALTT-ABX") and asked to make the Day 7 dependency explicit: submit retireOldStakingVault() only after upgradeFolio() has executed. tbrent corrected the typo.

tbrent opened community polls on rebalancing before deprecation for four indices:

  • CLX (https://forum.reserve.org/t/1766): into 100% USDC, into a less-concentrated basket (not 50/29/15), or No.
  • DFX (https://forum.reserve.org/t/1767): into 100% USDC, Yes or No.
  • BGCI (https://forum.reserve.org/t/1769): into 100% USDC, Yes or No. tbrent noted BGCI holds all bridged tokens as collateral, so deprecating before rebalancing out could cost users funds if bridge tokens lose support.
  • DGI (https://forum.reserve.org/t/1768): into 100% USDC, Yes or No.

All four polls had 0 votes in this window.

On the eUSD Revenue Share Programme Update (https://forum.reserve.org/t/1664), Raphael_Anode (Anode) suggested automation push only IPs above a 3% relative change so no manual review is needed, or automate revenue share without IPs. This is pending development, but could be set so 90% of yield goes to FinTechs prorated to holdings.

On the RFC to add the UglyCash address to eUSD revenue share (https://forum.reserve.org/t/1678), a reviewer asked whether the addition is purely prospective and if not, what effective date and adjustment apply.

New topics

None.

Discussion by topic

ABX proposal-spam upgrade: supportive, with two editorial concerns (a label typo and a sequencing dependency) both acknowledged. The upgrade passed and executed.

Deprecations CLX, DFX, BGCI and DGI: tbrent is surveying the community before any deprecation. No votes cast. BGCI carries the main substantive concern over all-bridged collateral.

eUSD revenue share: collaborative refinement between Anode and reviewers on automation and the UglyCash address.

Sentiment

Supportive and open. The only criticism was editorial on the ABX thread and was resolved. The deprecation and revenue share threads are awaiting community and author input rather than showing opposition.

What to watch

  • Rebalancing polls for CLX, DFX, BGCI and DGI are open with no votes yet; results may inform deprecation sequencing.
  • ABX proposal-spam protection upgrade has executed onchain.
  • The UglyCash address question (retroactive or prospective) on topic 1678 awaits an author response before a vote.
