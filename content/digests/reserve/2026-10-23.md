## Discussion by topic

Topic 1669, eUSD staking and overcollateralization:

- Raphael_Anode (Anode) opened the RFC. Following the Q3 community call and the prior eUSD revenue-sharing discussion, it invites views on what a revised model should achieve, raising several connected questions: how much RSR backing eUSD needs, what kind of yield attracts further backing, what yield maintains the current level of backing, and how much revenue fintechs need to grow distribution.

Topic 1670, eUSD Revenue Share Programme Update:

- ham opened the proposal. It updates the existing ongoing proposal of sharing eUSD revenue with participating fintechs, Ugly Cash and Sentz. If enacted, it would update the Revenue Share to maintain compliance with the rules of the original revenue share proposal and the subsequent update that redirected 10% of Fintech Revenues to stRSR holders.
