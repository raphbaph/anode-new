## Proposals and RFCs
### CMC20 + ixEdel optimistic governance upgrade (1.1.0) (topic 1694)
tbrent announced the ixEdel proposal is live and the CMC20 proposal is live, both to be voted on in the Reserve app under DTFs. https://forum.reserve.org/t/1694

### Proposal-spam protection upgrade for vlRSR governance (topic 1661)
tbrent confirmed the second round of proposals is live for PHOTON, NEOCLOUD, POWER, BUILDOUT, ROBOTS and MAG7. These upgrade the DTF governance systems, not the DAO or staking vault side which is already done, from 1.0.0 to 1.1.0. The upgrades are in-place. https://forum.reserve.org/t/1661

### Shared vlRSR Proposal Thresholds (topic 1695)
tbrent announced the vlRSR 1% proposalThreshold proposals are live on Mainnet, Base and BSC. The Mainnet proposal is not currently used by any vaults, though ixEdel will use it soon. The DTF 1% proposalThresholds proposals are also live for PHOTON, NEOCLOUD, POWER, BUILDOUT, ROBOTS and MAG7. Other DTF governors do not need the 1% proposal bump, as their 1.1.0 upgrades include 1% by default. https://forum.reserve.org/t/1695
