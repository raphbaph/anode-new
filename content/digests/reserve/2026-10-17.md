## Proposals and RFCs

Two new RFCs opened in the Governance and Delegates category, both by tbrent, proposing upgrades to Reserve Governor 1.1.0 for proposal-spam protection.

### [RFC] Proposal-spam protection upgrade for OPEN
https://forum.reserve.org/t/1666

Proposes upgrading OPEN governance on Ethereum to Reserve Governor 1.1.0. The purpose is proposal-spam protection. Unlike the vlRSR upgrade, this requires a new staking vault and migration of existing stake. OPEN will continue using its existing governance token SQUILL, not RSR.

### [RFC] Proposal-spam protection upgrade for ABX
https://forum.reserve.org/t/1667

Proposes upgrading ABX governance on Base to Reserve Governor 1.1.0. The purpose is proposal-spam protection. This also requires a new staking vault and migration of existing stake. ABX will continue using its existing governance token ALTT, not RSR.
