Raphael_Anode (Anode) opened a new topic aggregating the three rounds of DTF deprecations run in 2026, plus the October governance upgrades, into one timeline (https://forum.reserve.org/t/1780). On the RFC to add the UglyCash address to eUSD revenue share (https://forum.reserve.org/t/1678), Raphael_Anode (Anode) clarified the intent is forward facing. Adding wallets requires no on-chain IP because the revenue split is calculated off-chain, and a post by an official FinTech member (UGLYCASH's Kari) is enough to include the wallet. The revenue share will be automated soon, after which wallet changes would likely require an IP.
