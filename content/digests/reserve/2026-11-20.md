## Proposals and RFCs
- [RFC] eUSD Revenue Share Programme Update 19-11-2026 ([topic 1775](https://forum.reserve.org/t/1775), eUSD RFC), posted by ham on 2026-11-19. It updates the existing Revenue Share Programme for participating fintechs, Ugly Cash and Sentz. The November 2026 amendment covers the tracked UGLYCASH wallets used within the revenue share programme. Per the policy it will advance to an implementation proposal only if any recipient's calculated revenue share differs from its current on-chain allocation by at least 3% in relative terms, in either direction, following the threshold agreed in an earlier poll. The post sets out a timeline (original June 2024 programme, March 2026 90/10 adjustment, October 2026 relative 3% threshold poll, November 2026 wallet amendment) and argues the update keeps revenue shares aligned with the biweekly snapshot cadence to preserve predictability for fintech participants and minimise governance overhead.

## Discussion by topic
**eUSD Revenue Share Programme Update** ([topic 1775](https://forum.reserve.org/t/1775), eUSD RFC). ham opened the topic and detailed the rationale: revenue predictability matters to fintechs, and drift in revenue shares from ratified allocations creates misalignment, so updates follow the existing programme rules on a biweekly cadence. He flagged that overcollateralisation depends on collateral basket APY, the amount of RSR staked, the price of RSR and the share of revenue directed to stRSR, and that the recent 90/10 split mitigates lower staking yields and strengthens incentives for RSR staking. Operational risks around fintech wallet submissions are mitigated by requiring fintechs to confirm submitted wallets. Raphael_Anode (Anode) replied thanking ham and pointing readers to the related discussion on eUSD staking and overcollateralisation and the eUSD economics simulator.
