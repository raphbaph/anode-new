## Discussion by topic

- Grants & Incentives, Andromeda Core (2606), https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/876

  Andromeda Core posted the final technical report and closure of the FES pilot. The observation window closed on 11 November 2026 at 08:45 UTC, 28 days after it opened. The frozen cohort of 18 wallets remained immutable from October 14 to November 11. Confirmed organic exposure was 0 of 18, with 0 attributed stakes and no conversion data. The verdict is inconclusive: the pre-registered exposure floor of 8 views was never reached, an outcome the grantee presents as a documented finding rather than a refutation of the hypothesis. No technical failure occurred. Two stakes were detected on-chain during the window, both pre-window fixtures and not counted. The grantee reported roughly 1,768 broadcast impressions against a universe of about 19,000 RIF holders, and estimated the expected reach into the 18-wallet cohort at approximately 1.7 wallets under the most favorable interpretation, which it says is why no cohort wallet was confirmed. Andromeda funded the entire pilot; the DAO has not been asked for, and has not paid, anything. No payment is being requested, and the proposal never went to an on-chain vote. The report lists public artifacts including an IPFS snapshot, a Merkle root and generation scripts on GitHub, plus an audit, test plan and requirements matrix. It closes without an outcome-condition claim and responds to questions raised earlier in the thread by Tane and by Curia about reach and distribution.
