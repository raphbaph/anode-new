## Discussion by topic

- [2611 Grant] BitChill: V2 audit and relaunch of non-custodial Bitcoin DCA (Grants & Incentives, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1022). IBUKI asked whether the keeper can still execute unaffected purchase schedules when a LayerBank withdrawal fails or a USDRIF swap hits its price limit. BitChill (Antonio) confirmed the keeper simulates each batch before sending, removes the schedules that cause a failure, sends the rest, and is notified on every such event, a practice used during Sovryn's liquidity crisis. IBUKI acknowledged the explanation.

- RPCDrift: Rootstock RPC Compatibility & Regression Testing (Grants & Incentives, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1011). Emmarobinson noted that receipt and transaction RPC responses depend on chain state, so comparing live mainnet runs can produce false positives, and suggested making M1 explicit that regression checks run against pinned block heights or captured fixtures, with a separate lightweight smoke test on live networks.

- [2601 Grant] SwaptoX Aggregator, Milestone 1 (Grants & Incentives, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/700). The team asked whether the on-chain proposal is intended for formal milestone review and acceptance or primarily for recording completion and the governance decision on-chain. The reply stated the primary purpose is to ratify completion and facilitate disbursement, since the DAO treasury is controlled by governance. Emmarobinson added that the on-chain proposal is the disbursement step rather than a second technical review, that forum review establishes completion, and that this should be kept separate from the two open points. For Milestone 4 emmarobinson suggested a partial holdback tied to the 60-day KPI review, sized so campaigns can run.

- General Guidelines for Grant Applications (Grants & Incentives, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/94). Raphael_Anode (Anode) described one application as definitely a great product and a good fit for Rootstock Collective, noting ongoing conversations with the applicant's team.
