## Proposals and RFCs

No new proposals this window. Two pre-AMA grants drew sustained delegate scrutiny.

Predge ([2610 Grant](https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1009), 35,000 USD Strategic tier) responded point by point to delegates: it dropped the self-integration fallback from Milestone 3, committed to a signed scope from an independent Rootstock team before any vote, narrowed the grant to one flow (deterministic checks for paid API calls and agent jobs), and published a 20 Oct self-review of its v1 contracts (3 high, 5 medium, 7 low, 7 informational). IBUKI, a Hiroshima demolition operator and Rootstock Ambassador, agreed to run a testnet PoC with sample construction contracts due by Nov 10. Nihar of Areta Market offered to source audit quotes. Predge said the grant has no confirmed timeline, so the PoC proceeds regardless of grant outcome with no costs or obligations for IBUKI.

RPCDrift ([topic](https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1011)) reduced its ask to a 6,750 USD ceiling at 30 USD per hour. Delegates (DAOstar_gov, Axia, ChronoTrigger, Eren_DAOplomats, SEEDGov, Manuvegadt, LEGOSI) uniformly require demand evidence, written interest from at least two Rootstock teams, to be posted before the Initial AMA rather than funded as a Milestone 1 deliverable, citing grant guidelines V3.3. Verification of the technical lead was also raised.

- Predge (t/1009): delegates questioned the core product versus first user, the audit scope, and demand validation. Predge narrowed scope and published the v1 review. The IBUKI PoC proceeds regardless of the grant.
- RPCDrift (t/1011): the budget cut was welcomed; the pre-AMA demand-evidence requirement remains unsatisfied. LEGOSI restated the gate: letters of intent from allied dApps plus public GitHub verification before voting.

SEEDGov liked and retweeted from the official X account. Manuvegadt and LEGOSI framed the near-zero result as distribution friction data rather than failure.
