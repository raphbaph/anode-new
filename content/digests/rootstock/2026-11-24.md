## Discussion by topic

### [2611 Grant] BitChill: V2 audit and relaunch of non-custodial Bitcoin DCA (https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1022)

BitChill answered Curia's key-person question. Getting V2 audited, deployed and running depends entirely on BitChill; once live, day-to-day operation is automated, the keeper runs weekly batches on its own and monitoring alerts on failure. A period without BitChill would not stop the service by itself; it would only matter if something broke while the applicant was away.

### [2610 Grant] Predge, disputable USDRIF payments on Rootstock without an escrow agent, Milestone 1 (https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1009)

predge answered santimirovasergij on why job 1 ran in tRBTC. The AgentJob contract in this proof of concept escrows the native coin via msg.value, which was the fastest way to get a live job on testnet. USDRIF is available on Rootstock testnet (RIF US Dollar at 0x8dbF326E12a9fF37ED6ddf75adA548c2640a6482, verified, around 226 holders), so there is no liquidity constraint. The plan is to add an ERC-20 path to AgentJob using approve and transferFrom.

### [2601 Grant] SwaptoX Aggregator, Milestone 1 (https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/700)

SwaptoX responded to feedback and addressed each point. On Tane's points about timelock, audit and Milestone 4 funding, the Router is currently managed by a single administrator; once ownership is transferred to the Timelock contract, the transfer cannot be reversed through the current ownership mechanism. The audit and Timelock discussion continues subject to the audit completing.
