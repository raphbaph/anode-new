## Discussion by topic

[2608 Grant Proposal] 1delta Intents, permissionless solving on Rootstock (topic 929, Grants and Incentives)
- Achthar announced the beta application is live at x-beta.1delta.io, with an analytics dashboard added to the page. It can swap USDT0 with no gas fees. Thanks were given to Curia, ChronoTrigger, DAOplomats, Tane, iamChekwube and LEGOSI for feedback.
- IBUKI asked whether the team will publish a simple explanation of how gasless swaps are executed, who covers the transaction fees, and what users should know about potential risks.
- Achthar replied that an article will be prepared. The mechanism uses erc20Permit (gasless approvals) together with intents, so the solver pays for both the approval and the execution gas, reflected in the quote.
- IBUKI thanked the team, noted the distinction between the gasless user experience and gas costs being included in the quote, and offered to help introduce the mechanism to the Japanese Rootstock community.

[2601 Grant] SwaptoX Aggregator, Milestone 1 (topic 700, Grants and Incentives)
- Achthar asked whether SwaptoX would run a solver, or provide an API, for the 1delta X intent solution.
- SwaptoX said the API and documentation will be public around the end of the month. The standard API is rate-limited to one request per second, and SwaptoX asked whether 1delta needs a higher rate limit or specific features.
