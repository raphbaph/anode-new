## Discussion by topic

- 2601 Grant SwaptoX Aggregator, Milestone 1 (Grants & Incentives, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/700). SwaptoX reported that improved network conditions cut API quote response times from 5 to 6 seconds to roughly 2 to 3 seconds from Asia, and shared a demonstration of two MetaMask swaps including USDRIF to WETH. Raphael_Anode (Anode) thanked SwaptoX for the Milestone 3 completion report and offered support moving it to an on-chain proposal. SwaptoX then asked whether moving the Milestone 3 completion report to an on-chain proposal is a new requirement, noting Milestones 1 and 2 were reviewed and approved in the forum before proceeding.

- 2611 Grant BitChill: V2 audit and relaunch of non-custodial Bitcoin DCA (Grants & Incentives, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1022). Mattsanto voiced support, calling the app exactly what the Rootstock ecosystem needs, and favoured the two audits, milestone payments and open reporting of past issues, while raising two points including that the M4 target looks low. BitChill replied that the roughly 15,000 per quarter M4 target is V1's first-year average, that V1's first quarter was about 10,200 DOC so the USD 10,000 unlocking full M4 approximates V1's same-period result, and that V2 starts from zero deposits with one lending venue and lower yields.

- SEEDGov Delegate Thread (Delegate Platforms, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/774). SEEDGov recorded a FOR vote on [GRANT 2611] Recognized Delegate Compensation, October 2026 Resubmission, with the rationale that the original proposal failed for lack of quorum and was resubmitted in exactly the same terms, and that SEEDGov favours distributing compensation to delegates.
