## Discussion by topic

- Grants & Incentives, Predge (2610), Milestone 1 dispute scope, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/1009

  A three post exchange refined the scope of the disputable-payment grant. A P2P trader with more than 50,000 operations since 2019, mostly in Venezuela, asked the grantee to make the P2P scope explicit on top of the audited, deterministic checks for paid API calls and agent jobs. The grantee agreed that P2P fiat trades are out of scope for the grant: whether a bank transfer arrived, or came from a third party's account, happens off-chain, and no pre-committed on-chain test can check it. Milestones 1 to 3 cover only what a machine can check against terms committed before payment. The trader then closed supportive, saying scoping P2P out keeps the grant honest about what can be verified on-chain, that the deadline helper and settlement breakdown should make the dispute side easier to trust, and offered to review the interface page in Spanish once it is live.

- Grants & Incentives, SwaptoX (2601), Milestone 3 completion report, https://gov.rootstockcollective.xyz/t/700
