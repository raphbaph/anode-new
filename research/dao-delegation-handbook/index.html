Inside the handbook

A framework for
delegated discretion.

What authority moves when a DAO delegates—and how can it retain control?

The handbook examines delegation as an authority relationship: who grants power, what it covers, how much judgment the recipient can exercise, how decisions are executed, and how that power can be withdrawn.

It follows those questions across token delegation, DReps, validators, expert councils, stewards, institutional custody workflows and AI-assisted systems. Cases illustrate mechanisms, trade-offs and redesigns rather than a universal model to copy.
